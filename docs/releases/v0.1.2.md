# Endure v0.1.2

This release carries the owner-controlled burn rate under protocol key `2043`. The economics remain experimental, and mainnet serving still needs the explicit operator acknowledgement.

- Package/API version: `0.1.2`
- Release tag: `v0.1.2`
- Protocol key: `2043`. It replaces `2042`, and **miners and validators must upgrade together**. A key-`2042` peer is rejected with `VERSION_MISMATCH`.
- Images: `ghcr.io/endure-network/endure-subnet-validator` and `ghcr.io/endure-network/endure-subnet-miner`, Linux/amd64.
- Channels: `:testnet` for qualified staging candidates; `:prod` and `:v0.1.2` for this release. Deploy by digest to pin exact image bytes.
- Chain parameters: **no `weights_version` change.** SN30's `weights_version` is `2040`, and Subtensor accepts any `version_key` at or above it, so key-`2043` submissions are accepted as-is. Scored votes also need chain `min_allowed_weights` of `1` and a `max_weight_limit` of `1` (u16 `65535`), which SN30 already has.

## What changed

The full change set, review history and evidence are in [#77](https://github.com/endure-network/endure-subnet/pull/77). See the [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md) for the itemised list.

- **Owner burn rate:**
  - On served mainnet SN30 and testnet, a scored weight attempt reads the subnet owner hotkey's commitment `endure.burn_bps=<0..10000>` at the emission snapshot block.
  - The owner UID receives that share of the vote, which the chain burns. Miners share the rest by earned weight.
  - A missing or malformed commitment burns the whole vote; `endure.burn_bps=0` pays the earned vector alone.
  - A new rate applies at each validator's next weight attempt, with no restart.
  - With no positive score, the owner vote is unchanged.
- **Withheld owner hotkeys:** the owner hotkey and every hotkey of the owner's coldkey earn nothing at any rate, because the chain withholds their incentive. Do not run miners under the owner coldkey.
- **Owner validity and rechecks:**
  - Scored votes on owner-vote networks require a valid owner: the pinned SN30 owner on mainnet.
  - Every scored vote is rechecked before sending, at any rate including zero. The chain must have `min_allowed_weights` and `max_weight_limit` of `1`, no withheld UID may carry weight, and the owner's share must be within rounding of the published rate.
  - A failed commitment read is retried in place up to three times, then abstains with `owner_commitment_unavailable` until the next attempt.
- **Owner tool:** `scripts/set_burn_rate.py` reads the current rate. With `--publish`, it publishes a new rate from the owner hotkey and reads it back.
- **Qualification:** localnet qualification now runs a burn cycle (`make devnet-burn-cycle`), using the devnet-only `--endure.devnet_owner_vote` opt-in. That flag is refused on the mock chain, testnet and mainnet.

This release adds no schema migration and no new dependency. The version bump leaves the protocol digest unchanged.

## Operator upgrade

Follow the [coordinated cutover](../running_on_mainnet.md#coordinated-cutover). In short:

1. **The owner publishes the burn rate first.** Key-`2042` validators ignore the commitment, and key-`2043` validators burn the whole vote until it exists:

   ```bash
   python scripts/set_burn_rate.py --network finney --netuid 30 \
       --wallet-name <owner wallet> --wallet-hotkey <owner hotkey> --publish <bps>
   ```

   The tool refuses to publish unless the signing hotkey is the subnet owner hotkey, and on mainnet unless it is the pinned SN30 owner.
2. **Back up the database** with a consistent SQLite backup, and keep the previous key-`2042` image references.
3. **Switch weight writers without overlap**, as for `v0.1.1`. Run exactly one writer per hotkey. The first vote lands after the startup fence and epoch pacing, roughly 300 blocks after start.
4. **Monitor `/health`:**
   - `emission_burn_bps` is the rate applied at the last scored attempt;
   - `emission_mode`, `emission_reason` and `last_confirmed_weights_at` work as before.

   On networks with commit-reveal enabled, a restart can leave the previous process's last weight commit unconfirmed. `/health` then reports degraded until the new process's first submission confirms. On testnet, after the 2026-10-03 deploy at 09:18 UTC, that lasted from 10:31 until the first key-`2043` vote confirmed at 12:58. SN30 has commit-reveal disabled, so its submissions confirm within blocks.

## Owner soak decision — 2026-10-05

The owner decided not to wait for a fresh seven-day window on the release commit. This is a scoped exception to the unchanged-revision rule.

The protocol code in this release first ran on testnet at staging commit `02ab118`, deployed on 2026-10-03 at 09:18 UTC. Over the following two days it met every checkpoint of the `v0.1.1` targeted soak:

- confirmed key-`2043` weight submissions, each giving the owner UID 98.00% at the published testnet rate `endure.burn_bps=9800`, with the owner-coldkey UIDs at zero;
- full round cycles on testnet: commits and reveals held, rounds resolving with no overdue horizon, and earned shares updating;
- a restart: the deploy itself, with a clean recovery.

The release commit adds only version metadata to that code. Its own soak is therefore targeted: roughly 24 hours on the **exact release commit**, the staging commit whose published `sha-<commit>` images the `v0.1.2` tag promotes. Its checkpoints are:

- a confirmed key-`2043` weight submission on testnet;
- one full round cycle on testnet (commit, reveal, resolution and scoring);
- a restart. The redeploy that carries this version bump to staging serves as the deliberate restart check.

After that, the owner publishes the SN30 burn rate. The release is then deployed first as a mainnet canary on the Endure team's own validator (SN30 UID 195), before third-party operators are asked to upgrade.

This document records the decision and its criteria only. The outcomes of each checkpoint and the canary are recorded in the GitHub release at tag time. CI and localnet qualification remain required for the selected staging commit.

The economic and independent-validator limitations in [economic limitations](../economic-limitations.md) remain disclosed and are not represented as resolved.

## Publication and rollback

Promote `develop` to `staging`, then `staging` to `main`, using merge commits. Tag the qualified staging commit reachable from `main`, because its `sha-<commit>` images are the inputs to production publication. The production workflow adds `:prod` and `:v0.1.2` to those recorded image manifests without rebuilding. Record both resulting digests in the GitHub release.

Coolify builds separately from the same source. Source identity and runtime content checks do not prove that its image digest equals the published image digest, and this release does not claim that equivalence.

This release adds no schema migration. Rolling back means returning *both* miners and validators to the key-`2042` `v0.1.1` images together, restoring the pre-upgrade database backup, and following the [operator rollback procedure](../deploy/operator-node.md#rollback). Key-`2042` validators ignore the owner commitment.
