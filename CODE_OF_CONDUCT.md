# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Endure contributors and operators are expected to be respectful, constructive,
and professional. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.
Give actionable feedback, assume good faith until evidence says otherwise, and
make space for people with different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, and the
disclosure of another person's private, credential, or wallet information are
prohibited. Deliberately unsafe advice about keys, funds, or mainnet operation is
also unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in the repository and in Endure-managed project spaces.
Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@endure.network`; do not include
wallet material or unrelated personal information. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to
protect the community. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as
practical, without promising a specific outcome or response time.
